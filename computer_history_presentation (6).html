Advancement of Computers

Computers have evolved significantly over the decades since they originated. Older computers were very large and slow.

Gradually, the size of computers became smaller but their speed became faster!

Fact File:
In 2015, Apple reduced computer size drastically with the launch of the Apple Watch!

Early Calculating Devices

The Abacus

Invented ~5000 years ago in China! Uses a wooden frame divided into Heaven (2 beads) and Earth (5 beads).

Pascaline

Invented in 1642 by Blaise Pascal to help his father. It used gears, dials, and wheels for addition and subtraction.

Leibniz Calculator

Invented in 1694. It was the first mechanical calculator that could perform +, -, ×, and ÷ using repeated addition.

The Pioneers of Computers

Charles Babbage

"Father of Computer"

Invented the Analytical Engine in 1833. Modern computers are based on his amazing five-unit design!

Lady Ada Lovelace

"First Programmer"

Wrote the first computer program. The early programming language 'Ada' was named to honor her.

Herman Hollerith

"Tabulating Machine"

Invented an electromechanical machine using punched cards to quickly process the 1890 U.S. Census!

1st Generation (1940-1956)

Vacuum Tubes

  • Used fragile Vacuum Tubes for circuitry.
  • They were very large, heavy, and produced a lot of heat.
  • Used punched cards and paper tapes for input.
  • Examples: ENIAC, EDVAC, UNIVAC.

2nd Generation (1956-1963)

Transistors

  • Used Transistors instead of heavy vacuum tubes.
  • Computers became drastically smaller, faster, and more reliable.
  • Used magnetic tapes for data storage.
  • Languages: COBOL, BASIC, FORTRAN.

3rd Generation (1964-1971)

Integrated Circuits

  • Used Integrated Circuits (ICs), known as Silicon chips.
  • Many miniaturized transistors were placed on a single chip.
  • Introduced keyboards and monitors for the first time!
  • Examples: IBM 370, Altair.

4th Generation (1971-Present)

Microprocessors

  • Uses Microprocessors (VLSI). A single chip contains a whole CPU!
  • Size became small enough to fit on a desk (Personal Computers).
  • Introduced the mouse and magnetic disks.
  • Languages: C++, PHP, Python.

5th Generation:
Present & Beyond

The fifth generation uses the exciting technology of Artificial Intelligence (AI).

  • Builds computers that mimic human behavior.
  • Includes Voice and Speech Recognition.
  • Used in medical areas, games, and entertainment.
