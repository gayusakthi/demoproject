The Smart Machine

Grade 2 Lesson

What is a Machine?

Machines make our work easier!

  • A car helps us travel.
  • A washing machine washes clothes.
  • A calculator does sums.

Most machines can do only one kind of work.

Car
Washing Machine
Calculator

Why is it a SMART Machine?

A computer is a machine too, but it is different!

It works very fast and saves our time!

It never feels tired or bored. It does not need rest.

It does not make mistakes.

It can remember a large amount of information and never forgets!

Because it can do MANY different things, we call it a SMART machine!

What can we do on a Computer?

We can do so many fun and useful things!

Type letters

Draw and paint

Play games

Listen to music

Watch movies

Solve sums

A computer runs on electricity. So, it is called an electronic machine.

Types of Computers

1. Desktop Computer

Big in size. Kept on a desk. Works on electricity.

2. Laptop Computer

Smaller than desktop. Keep it on your lap! Carried easily. Works on batteries.

3. Tablet Computer

Smaller than laptop. Has a touchscreen to give orders. Works on batteries.

4. Smartphone

Mobile phone with a computer inside. Call, message, play, and search!
